A Few Helpful Answers

Service FAQs

Here are a few things to know before choosing your service.

How do I choose the right package?

Consider your vehicle's current condition and your goals. Enhance Gold is intended as a maintenance-focused starting point, Enhance Elite offers a more comprehensive cleaning outline, and Enhance Showroom is intended for a more intensive, customized experience. We will help determine the appropriate scope before confirming your quote.

Can I book an individual service without a package?

Yes. You can request a specialty service on its own. A minimum charge applies to standalone mobile appointments. Multiple services can be combined into one quote rather than automatically charging the minimum separately for every service.

Are specialty services included in the package prices?

Not automatically. Services such as heavy pet-hair removal, odor treatment, engine-bay detailing, headlight restoration, wheel polishing, and paint-related treatments must be specifically included in the quote to be considered part of the agreed service.

Why might my final price be higher than the starting price?

The final price may vary based on vehicle size, condition, the time and labor required, requested specialty services, and applicable travel charges. We will discuss the scope and applicable charges with you before you approve the work.

Do you guarantee that every stain or odor will disappear?

No. Results depend on the material, the source and age of the stain or odor, previous treatments, and the condition of the vehicle. We will explain reasonable expectations before beginning specialized treatments.

When is my appointment confirmed?

Sending a service request does not itself confirm an appointment. Your required deposit must be received and we must send confirmation. Please review the full booking policies for the applicable deposit amounts and other terms.

Read the full booking policies →

Where do you provide mobile detailing?

Our service area is centered around Alameda and includes surrounding Bay Area communities. Locations outside the standard service radius may require an additional travel charge. Please provide your service address when requesting a quote.
