Specialty services

Details That Make a Difference.

Specialty work may be added to a suitable package or quoted separately. We review the vehicle's condition and the requested scope before confirming pricing.

Headlight Restoration

Improve the appearance of cloudy or oxidized headlights. Results depend on lens condition and the restoration method required.

Engine Bay Detail

Careful cleaning of appropriate engine-bay surfaces using methods suited to the vehicle and its condition.

Stain Removal

Targeted treatment for interior spots and stains. Results vary with material, stain type, and how long the stain has been present.

Mold & Mildew Assessment

Moisture-related concerns require assessment before work is accepted. Extensive contamination may require a qualified remediation specialist.

Wheel Polishing

For suitable wheel finishes; typically quoted at $50–$100 per set depending on condition and finish.

Trim Restoration

Refresh faded exterior trim where the material and condition allow.

Pet Hair Removal

Additional labor for stubborn or heavily embedded pet hair.

Heavy Soil Treatment

Extra attention for unusually dirty interiors and accumulated grime.

Odor Treatment

Targeted treatment based on the odor source and affected interior materials.

Paint Correction

Quoted separately after reviewing paint condition, expectations, and the level of correction requested.
