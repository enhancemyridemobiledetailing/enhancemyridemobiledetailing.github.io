Good to Know

Booking Questions

A few important details before you request your appointment.

Is my appointment confirmed when I select a time?

No. Your selected time is a request. We must first review the job and availability, agree on the quote, receive the required deposit, and send confirmation.

When do I pay the deposit?

After we review the job and you approve the quote, we will send a payment request for the required deposit. This booking page does not itself collect that deposit.

Can the final price be different from the package price?

Yes. Advertised prices are starting prices. Vehicle size, condition, pet hair, stains, and additional services can affect the final agreed quote.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

One complimentary reschedule is available with at least 24 hours' notice, subject to availability. Please review the full booking policies for additional details.

What is the minimum price for a standalone service?

The minimum charge for a standalone mobile appointment is $100. Additional services requested during a package appointment are quoted separately as applicable.

Where can I read the complete policies?

Visit our Booking Policies page for the full terms, including deposits, rescheduling, cancellations, and other booking conditions.
