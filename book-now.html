Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions before you schedule.

Does booking a Calendly time confirm my detailing appointment?

No. Calendly schedules your phone consultation. Your detailing date, final quote, deposit, and service confirmation are arranged separately.

When is the deposit due?

After we review the job and you approve the quote, we'll send a payment request for the required deposit. This page does not collect the deposit.

Can the final price differ from the package starting price?

Yes. Vehicle size, condition, pet hair, stains, contamination, and requested services may affect the final agreed quote.

Can I reschedule?

One complimentary reschedule is available with at least 24 hours' notice, subject to availability. Please review the full booking policies for additional details.

What is the minimum charge for a standalone service?

The minimum charge for a standalone mobile appointment is $100. Additional services requested during a package appointment are quoted separately as applicable.

Where can I read the full policies?

Visit our Booking Policies page for complete terms, including deposits, rescheduling, and cancellations.
