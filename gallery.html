Specialty Service

Headlight Restoration.

Cloudy, oxidized headlights can detract from a vehicle's appearance. Headlight restoration focuses on improving clarity and presentation where the lens condition allows.

We'll assess the headlight material and condition before recommending an appropriate treatment. The achievable result depends on the lens and the extent of deterioration.

Specialty Service

Engine Bay Detailing.

A carefully performed engine bay detail can help refresh the appearance of accessible engine-compartment surfaces and remove accumulated grime.

Cleaning methods are selected with care around sensitive components, electrical connections, and the condition of the vehicle. Not every engine bay is suitable for the same cleaning approach.

Interior Specialty Care

Stain, Mold & Mildew Treatment.

Interior stains and moisture-related concerns require careful assessment. We'll review the affected material, the size of the area, and the likely source before determining whether detailing treatment is appropriate.

Some stains may be permanent, and suspected mold or extensive mildew may require specialist assessment or remediation beyond routine automotive detailing. We do not promise complete mold removal or guarantee that an underlying moisture problem will be resolved through detailing alone.
