Booking Policies

We want every customer to understand the appointment process before committing to a service. Please read these policies carefully before requesting, confirming, or changing an appointment.

01 Booking Confirmation

Submitting a booking request, contacting us, or receiving a preliminary estimate does not by itself reserve an appointment.

An appointment is not confirmed until the required deposit has been received and Enhance My Ride has sent confirmation.

Before confirmation, we will work with you to establish the service scope, agreed price, appointment date and time, service location, and applicable deposit. Please do not assume that your requested time is reserved until you receive our confirmation.

Keep your booking confirmation and any written agreement about the services, price, and appointment changes for your records.

02 Deposits & Payment

A deposit is required to confirm a booking. The required deposit is based on the total agreed job price.

Agreed Job Price Required Deposit
$300 or less $50
Above $300 20% of the agreed price
Example: $250 job $50 deposit. The remaining $200 is due according to the agreed payment terms.
Example: $500 job $100 deposit. The remaining $400 is due according to the agreed payment terms.

Important Deposit Terms

  • The deposit is applied toward the agreed total price unless otherwise stated in a written agreement.
  • The deposit amount and payment instructions will be communicated before you pay.
  • Any remaining balance, approved additional work, and other agreed charges must be paid according to the terms communicated for your appointment.
  • We will not add unapproved extra services or charges without discussing them with you.

Deposits are intended to secure appointment time and prepare for the agreed service. Refund eligibility, including any applicable cancellation rights, is subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the booking.

03 Cancellations & Refunds

Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel an appointment. Early notice gives us a better opportunity to adjust our schedule.

Our business policy is that deposits are generally nonrefundable when permitted by law. However, this policy does not remove any refund, cancellation, or other rights that may apply under California law or other applicable law.

  • Contact us directly to communicate a cancellation and retain a record of your message.
  • If we must cancel or cannot provide the agreed service, we will communicate with you about appropriate next steps.
  • If a refund or other remedy is required by law, we will comply with the applicable requirement.
  • A rescheduling request is not the same as a confirmed rescheduled appointment; the new date must be agreed upon.

Nothing in this policy is intended to waive or restrict a customer's rights under applicable law. Legal cancellation periods or refund requirements may apply in some circumstances.

04 Rescheduling

We understand that circumstances change. We offer one complimentary reschedule when you provide at least 24 hours' notice , subject to availability.

  • Contact us at least 24 hours before your scheduled appointment to request your complimentary reschedule.
  • The replacement date and time must be mutually agreed upon and available in our schedule.
  • Requests made with less than 24 hours' notice, repeated changes, or missed appointments may require a new agreement and may affect future booking arrangements, subject to applicable law.
  • If weather, safety, vehicle access, or another significant circumstance prevents service, we will discuss available options with you.

Please wait for confirmation of the new date and time before treating a rescheduled appointment as finalized.

05 Quotes, Pricing & Additional Work

Advertised package prices are starting prices, not a guarantee that every vehicle will qualify for the starting rate. Your quote will reflect the service scope agreed upon for your vehicle.

Factors that may affect the quote include:

  • Vehicle size and configuration.
  • Interior or exterior condition.
  • Heavy soil, excessive pet hair, or significant staining.
  • Specialty treatments or additional requested services.
  • Service location and any disclosed travel charge.

We will communicate applicable charges and the agreed service scope before beginning work. If we discover an issue that materially changes the work required, we will discuss the options and any price adjustment with you before proceeding with the additional work.

Please review our services and package descriptions for the current starting prices and available service options.

06 Standalone Services & Minimum Charge

Individual services may be requested without purchasing a complete detailing package, subject to availability and whether the requested work is suitable for your vehicle.

The minimum charge for a standalone mobile appointment is $100.

  • The minimum applies to a standalone mobile appointment, not automatically to each individual service included in a combined quote.
  • Multiple requested services can be assessed together and quoted as one appointment.
  • An add-on requested alongside an existing package is quoted at its applicable additional price; the standalone minimum is not automatically added just because an add-on is requested.
  • Travel charges, if applicable, will be disclosed before you approve the quote.

07 Arrival, Access & Vehicle Preparation

To help us complete your appointment efficiently, please provide accurate location and vehicle details and make the vehicle accessible at the agreed time.

  • Tell us about parking restrictions, access codes, gate instructions, and any location limitations before the appointment.
  • Remove valuable personal items and loose belongings from areas you want detailed.
  • Inform us about known vehicle issues, sensitive materials, prior damage, or other conditions that could affect the requested work.
  • Make sure the service location is safe and that we have the agreed access to the vehicle.

If the vehicle or location is materially different from what was described, we may need to revise the quote or reschedule after discussing the situation with you.

08 Service Limitations & Results

Every vehicle and surface is different. We select cleaning methods based on the vehicle's materials, condition, and the agreed service.

  • Some stains, odors, scratches, oxidation, and other defects may be permanent or only partially improved.
  • Cleaning and detailing are cosmetic services and do not constitute mechanical repair.
  • Paint correction, polishing, restoration, and other specialized procedures must be specifically discussed and quoted.
  • We will not knowingly proceed with a treatment that we consider inappropriate for a surface or that presents an unreasonable risk of damage.

We will explain relevant limitations where reasonably identifiable before performing the applicable treatment. Nothing in this section limits rights or responsibilities that cannot legally be excluded.
