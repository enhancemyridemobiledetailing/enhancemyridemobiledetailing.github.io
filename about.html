About Enhance My Ride

Vehicle care that fits your day.

Your time matters. Mobile detailing means you can get on with your day while we focus on your vehicle. Whether you want to refresh your daily driver, improve the appearance of your interior, or give your vehicle more thorough attention, we want the process to feel convenient and straightforward.

We begin by understanding your priorities. We'll discuss the vehicle's condition, the areas you'd like us to focus on, and which available services may suit your goals.

No two vehicles are exactly alike. The appropriate service depends on the vehicle, its current condition, and the result you're looking for. Our aim is to make those options clear before work begins.

What You Can Expect

Professional service starts with clear expectations.

We want you to understand what's included, what may affect pricing, and what to expect before your appointment gets underway.

  • ✓ Mobile service at an agreed location.
  • ✓ Three starting package levels to explore.
  • ✓ Optional services based on your vehicle's needs.
  • ✓ Clear communication about the requested work and pricing.
  • ✓ Service coverage centered around Alameda and surrounding Bay Area communities.

Vehicle size, heavy soil, pet hair, stains, and requested services can affect the time required and final price. Package prices are starting prices. We'll review the details and confirm the quote before beginning the agreed work.
